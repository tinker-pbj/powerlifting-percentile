How it's calculated

Who you're compared with: everyone in the OpenPowerlifting database who competed raw (no wraps, suits or shirts) in a drug-tested full-power meet, aged 16 or older (or age unknown), with no disqualification. Each lifter counts once, at their best ever result for that lift, using the bodyweight they weighed in at for that meet. That's 230k men and 110k women.

Your percentile is the share of those lifters whose best is below yours, with lifters near your bodyweight counting most:

percentile(x, b) = Σ wᵢ·[liftᵢ < x] / Σ wᵢ,   wᵢ = exp(−½·((ln bwᵢ − ln b) / h)²)

The weight is a bell curve on log bodyweight, so it's "within a few percent of your bodyweight" whether you're 52 kg or 140 kg. h = 0.035 (about ±3.5% of bodyweight). Where data is thin (very light or very heavy lifters), h widens until at least 1,500 lifters effectively count.

This is a percentile among people who compete, which is already a strong crowd. Among the general gym population, you'd rank much higher.
