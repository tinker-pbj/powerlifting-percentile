How these are calculated

From OpenPowerlifting / OpenWeightlifting meet results, raw and drug-tested only. Each lifter counts once, at their best result, and lifters closer to 100 kg bodyweight count more (a bell curve about ±3.5% wide). Percentiles are among people who compete, a strong crowd.
